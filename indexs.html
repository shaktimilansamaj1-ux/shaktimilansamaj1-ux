Shakti Milan Samaj, on 18th May 2026 conducted a Consultation and Discussion Meeting with ART counsellors, health service providers and relevant CSOs to ensure smooth implementation of health-related services for people living with HIV/AIDS.

Shakti Milan Samaj, on 18th May 2026 conducted a Consultation and Discussion Meeting with ART counsellors, health service providers and relevant CSOs to ensure smooth implementation of health-related services for people living with HIV/AIDS. The consultation meeting concluded with a collective recognition that despite the continued commitment of health institutions and service providers, significant challenges remain in ensuring consistent viral load testing, follow-up, and comprehensive care for people living with HIV, particularly among key populations and clients outside Kathmandu Valley, highlighting the urgent need for strengthened coordination, sustained funding, decentralized services, and collaborative interventions among all stakeholders. We are beyond grateful to have implemented this activity in collaboration with Cruiseaids Nepal and are thankful to Samata Bam for meaningful facilitation of the session. #hivawareness #Stigma #discrimination
